# Privacy Policy — Speakoza – English Practice

**Last Updated:** September 17, 2026

Speakoza ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, and protect your information when you use our mobile application **Speakoza – English Practice**.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### Account Information
When you register for Speakoza, we collect:
- Account email address
- User name or display handle
- Onboarding learning preferences (English proficiency level, learning goals, explanation language, daily practice target)

### Voice & Audio Data
- **Microphone Access**: Used solely while you actively press and hold/tap the microphone button during an active speaking session.
- **Audio Processing**: Speech audio is transcribed into text in real time using local speech-to-text engines or secure API endpoints.
- **No Permanent Raw Voice Storage**: Speakoza **does not permanently store your raw audio voice recordings** on server infrastructure unless explicitly disclosed for a specific requested feature.

### Transcripts & AI Feedback Data
- Text transcripts of your speaking practice
- AI feedback metrics (estimated scores for grammar, vocabulary, fluency, pronunciation, naturalness)
- Practice streak counts and daily challenge completion history

---

## 2. How We Use Information

We use collected information strictly to:
- Provide real-time AI English speaking practice and feedback
- Store your practice streak and progress statistics locally and securely on our backend database
- Personalize recommendations based on your selected goals and English level
- Ensure compliance with usage limits and secure service operation

---

## 3. Data Protection & Security

- **Row Level Security (RLS)**: User data is isolated. Each user can only view and edit their own profile, conversation history, and feedback.
- **No Third-Party Ad Tracking**: Speakoza does not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.
- **Secure Processing**: AI requests are securely proxied through backend APIs without exposing credentials.

---

## 4. Account & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data:
- **In-App Deletion**: Go to **Profile → Settings → Delete Account**. Confirm deletion to purge all profile metrics, conversation history, and usage records associated with your user ID.
- **Web Deletion Request**: You may also request deletion via email at `support@speakoza.app` or via our web deletion portal.
