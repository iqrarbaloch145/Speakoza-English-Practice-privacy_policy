Privacy Policy for Speakoza

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Speakoza ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our AI English Speaking Coach application collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Voice Data & Audio Recording: During speech practice sessions, your device microphone captures audio input to transcribe your spoken English sentences into text for AI analysis. Voice recordings are processed transiently for transcription and scoring.
  • Account Information: If you register an account, we collect your email address and basic profile details.
  • Usage & Practice Statistics: We store practice session logs, speech scores, vocabulary progress, and session history to provide personalized learning insights.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To evaluate fluency, grammar, pronunciation, and sentence naturalness.
  • To generate personalized AI coaching feedback and sentence corrections.
  • To display progress history and achievement stats across your learning journey.

3. Data Protection & Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or voice recordings to third parties. Audio streams and transcripts are transmitted securely using industry-standard SSL encryption.

4. User Rights & Account Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can request permanent deletion of your account, practice history, and stored data directly within the app under Profile → Settings → Delete Account & Data, or by contacting support.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or privacy concerns regarding Speakoza, please contact us at support@speakoza.com.
